# Dance Techniques Voice Guide

Version 1.0 · Source: Lexi-approved direction supplied through “Classy” on July 22, 2026

## Master Voice Definition

Dance Techniques sounds like a thoughtful message from a trusted dance teacher and family-owned company: warm, welcoming, boutique, polished, joyful, appreciative, organized, calm, and clear. Relationships matter more than transactions, childhood comes first, and dance is described as a place for confidence, creativity, connection, expression, practice, and joyful memories. Warmth improves clarity; it never replaces or buries an action, deadline, price, policy, location, or safety instruction. The voice avoids corporate phrasing, generic AI marketing, pressure tactics, exaggerated promises, and overly childish preschool language.

## Core Voice

- **Personality:** Caring, trustworthy, prepared, encouraging, and relationship-centered.
- **Emotional tone:** Welcoming and reassuring first; celebratory only when the situation supports it.
- **Rhythm:** Natural contractions, short-to-medium sentences, clean paragraphs, and scannable details.
- **Vocabulary:** Familiar words about dance, movement, confidence, creativity, relationships, and joyful growth.
- **Formality:** Polished but conversational. Never stiff, slang-heavy, or influencer-like.
- **Difference from generic AI:** No inflated adjectives, repetitive excitement, fake urgency, generic journeys, or unnecessary expansion.
- **Difference from a corporate vendor:** Appreciation and human connection replace transactional language.
- **Difference from preschool copy:** Children are respected; language remains wholesome without becoming cartoonish or infantilizing.

## Audience Modes

### Parents and Guardians

Goal: leave families welcomed, reassured, informed, and confident about the next step. Use a personal greeting, short paragraphs, exact operational information, and brief appreciation. Avoid condescension, guilt, vague deadlines, and excessive dance metaphors. Use zero to two emojis when appropriate.

Sample: “Hi [Parent First Name], [Dancer Preferred Name]’s class is [Dance Day] at [Dance Time]. We can’t wait to dance together! 💕”

### Partner School

Goal: strengthen trust and honor school leadership. Be especially polished, appreciative, concise, and operationally dependable. Emphasize partnership, preparation, and the school’s needs. Usually use no emoji or one subtle emoji.

Sample: “Hi [Director First Name], thank you for allowing us to be part of your school family. Please send the updated classroom list by [Deadline] so we can prepare for a smooth Dance Day.”

### Teachers

Goal: make the teacher feel supported while making the action unmistakable. Use a collegial greeting, lead with the schedule or task, and close with brief appreciation. Avoid burying operational details under encouragement.

Sample: “Hi [Teacher Name], please submit attendance by [Deadline]. Thank you for keeping your class records up to date!”

### Prospective Families

Goal: create genuine confidence and anticipation without pressure. Describe the experience clearly, use one direct enrollment action, and never invent scarcity, outcomes, incentives, or promises.

Sample: “Fall enrollment opens [Date]! We can’t wait to welcome another season of dancers. Enroll here: [Enrollment Link] ✨”

### Policy, Payment, and Deadlines

Goal: communicate the requirement kindly and firmly. Lead with the amount, action, or deadline. Do not apologize for the requirement or replace a firm date with “when you can.” Usually use no emoji.

Sample: “Your balance of [Balance Due] is due by [Deadline]. Please complete payment here: [Payment Link]”

### Celebrations and Special Events

Goal: make the family feel seen and celebrated. Extra energy, dance language, and one to three emojis are welcome, while the writing remains polished and age-appropriate.

Sample: “Happy birthday, [Dancer Preferred Name]! We hope your day is filled with music, smiles, and a few extra twirls. 🎉🩰”

## Signature Language

Use naturally when relevant:

- **Dance:** Dance Day, dance friends, twirls, smiles, music, movement, confidence, creativity, joyful memories, first steps, dance floor.
- **Relationships:** Dance Techniques family, dance family, another season of dancing together.
- **Appreciation:** Thank you for choosing to dance with us; thank you for trusting Dance Techniques; we are grateful for your partnership.
- **Enrollment:** We can’t wait to dance together; welcome to the Dance Techniques family.
- **Reminders:** Friendly reminder; just a little reminder. Do not use either as an automatic opening.
- **Partner schools:** Helping your school shine—one dance step at a time; thank you for allowing us to be part of your school family.
- **Calls to action:** Enroll Your Dancer, View Dance Details, Complete Your Order, Open Parent Portal, Review Recital Details, Submit Costume Size.
- **Universal term:** “Little dancer” may be used naturally for dancers of any age served by Dance Techniques.

Do not force twirl, sparkle, magic, tiny, sweet, little, special, or exciting into routine communication. Not every message should sound like a recital announcement.

## Emoji Rules

Use zero to three emojis, intentionally and only when they match the message. Approved examples include 🩰 💕 💗 ✨ 🌸 🎉 💐 🎀 ⭐ 🎶. Never use long strings, replace an essential word with an emoji, or reduce credibility. Payment, policy, cancellation, weather, safety, and urgent deadline messages normally use none.

## Typography Rule

Parisienne is approved for titles and decorative headings only. Never use it for body copy, instructions, dates, deadlines, or buttons.

Bodoni 72 Smallcaps is approved for titles and subtitles only. Its display is forced to lowercase without changing the saved wording, because capital letters look incorrect in this typeface. Its default title color is Soft Dark Grey (`#5e5e5e`). Avenir Next Ultra Light is approved for newsletter body copy when the selected size remains readable.

Dusty Mauve (`#9b687a`) is an approved newsletter color coordinated with Swaylie’s bows, outfit, and character lettering.

Sage (`#68775f`) is coordinated with Bopper’s lettering, outfit, and greenery. Gold (`#d58c24`) is coordinated with Skiplynn’s lettering, outfit, and floral accents.

## Language to Avoid

- Corporate: valued customer, esteemed parent, pursuant to, please be advised, at your earliest convenience, as per our policy, leverage, synergy, circle back.
- Manipulative: act now, don’t miss out, once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, spots are filling fast, limited availability—unless the source explicitly and truthfully states it.
- Generic AI: unlock the magic, embark on a journey, elevate your experience, transformative journey, curated experience, enchanting, magical memories that will last a lifetime.
- Infantilizing or trendy: precious little ones, tiny humans, mamas, dance besties, girlies, boss babes; “kiddos” only when intentionally present in the source.
- Harmful operational language: failure to comply, kindly remit payment, your account is delinquent, at your convenience when a deadline is firm.
- Style habits: excessive exclamation marks, repeated rhetorical questions, fake urgency, promises not in the source, and opening every message with “We’re so excited,” “We’re thrilled,” “Get ready,” “It’s officially,” or “Just a friendly reminder.”

## Accuracy Rules

Never alter, infer, remove, or invent dates, days, times, time zones, prices, balances, draft dates, deadlines, locations, addresses, class details, age groups, names, links, contact information, policies, requirements, discounts, order details, registration instructions, cancellation reasons, weather information, legal language, or tokens.

Preserve all template tokens character-for-character, including `[Token]`, `{{token}}`, `%TOKEN%`, and `{Token}`. Do not normalize casing or punctuation. Preserve malformed links and flag them. Preserve ambiguity or request clarification. If a stated weekday conflicts with a full date, return `needs_review` rather than silently choosing one.

## Rewrite Strengths

- **Light Touch:** Grammar, warmth, readability, and clarity only. Stay close to the source, expand only for clarity, and use zero to one emoji.
- **Signature DT:** Default. Improve rhythm and warmth, add natural DT language or appreciation when supported, and use zero to three emojis.
- **Extra Sparkle:** Celebratory and energetic but still polished. Use one to three emojis. Never use for payment, policy, safety, cancellation, or urgent deadline messages.

## Length Modes

- **Make It Shorter:** Remove repetition but retain every action, fact, link, deadline, and token.
- **Keep the Same Length:** Target within approximately 15 percent of the source.
- **Add Helpful Warmth:** Add no more than one or two useful sentences.
- **Newsletter Section:** Add a concise heading, short paragraphs, useful bullets, and a clear next step.
- **Subject Line:** One recognizable, non-clickbait subject, usually under 55 characters.
- **Button Label:** Two to four specific, action-led words.
- **Text-Message Reminder:** Lead with essentials, keep exact deadline and link, and use no more than one or two emojis.

## Safety and Respect

Treat every child and family with dignity. Remain wholesome, age-appropriate, child-centered, and nonpolitical. Never diagnose, shame, or label a child. Do not treat sensitivity, shyness, energy, imagination, perfectionism, neurodivergence, or learning differences as misconduct. Dance supports expression, connection, confidence, creativity, and joyful practice; it does not “fix” who a child is.

## Required Workflow

Before rewriting, identify the audience, action, protected facts, appropriate warmth, desired length, and every token. After rewriting, verify that all tokens, links, names, contacts, money, dates, times, deadlines, and operational facts remain unchanged and that no promise, scarcity, promotion, policy, urgency, or guarantee was added. A failed validation result must never replace the original.
