Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Dance Techniques LLC (“Dance Techniques,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides dance instruction and the My Dance Techniques Parent Portal. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information provided through our services.

Information we collect

We may collect parent or guardian contact information, including names, email addresses, and mobile phone numbers; dancer and enrollment information; account credentials; communications; attendance information; and billing records needed to provide our services.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure Parent Portal accounts, verify identity, communicate about classes and account activity, manage enrollments, support families, process authorized payments, and comply with legal obligations.

SMS and mobile information

When a parent or guardian requests a verification code, Dance Techniques LLC may send an automated one-time passcode to the mobile number entered. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver and secure requested messages, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Service providers and disclosures

We may use trusted providers for hosting, authentication, messaging, payments, analytics, and support. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our users, services, or legal rights.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of eligible personal information by contacting Dance Techniques. You may stop verification texts by replying STOP. Opting out of SMS may prevent phone-based account verification.

Contact us

Questions about this policy may be sent through the contact information published at dancetechniques.info.
